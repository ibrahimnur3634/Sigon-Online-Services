GOOD TO KNOW

Pricing questions

Are the displayed prices inclusive of government fees?

No. Any confirmed price is for Sigon’s service assistance only. Government and third-party fees are separate and will be clarified before you proceed.

How do I request a service?

Choose a service and select “Request Service.” WhatsApp opens with a message naming your selected service so you can ask about its price and requirements.

Can I request a custom service?

Yes. Contact us with a short description of what you need and we will review it and provide a quote where possible.

How do I make payments?

We will confirm the service scope, applicable charges and available payment instructions with you before work begins. Do not send payment details or verification codes in a service request.

Are all services available immediately?

Availability depends on the service requirements, information provided and the availability of relevant platforms or third parties. We will confirm timing when you contact us.

Can prices change?

Yes. A quote may vary with the complexity and scope of work or applicable third-party charges. We will confirm the amount with you before proceeding.
